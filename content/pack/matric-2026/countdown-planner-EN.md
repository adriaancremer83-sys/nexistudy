# THE 7-WEEK COUNTDOWN PLANNER
### NexiStudy Matric Prelim Survival Pack
**13 July – 29 August · The one that goes on the fridge**

---

## HOW THIS PLANNER WORKS

You cannot study everything. Nobody can. The learners who walk into prelims calm are not the ones who worked the most hours — they are the ones who spent their hours on the right things. This planner exists to make that decision for you, week by week, so you never sit down wondering *what* to study.

**The daily rhythm (every study day):**
- **2–3 blocks of 50 minutes**, with a 10-minute break between blocks. Phone in another room during blocks — not on silent, in another room.
- After each block, one line in your Gap Log (below): what you practised, what still leaks marks.
- **One full rest day every week.** Not negotiable. The Parents' Guide explains why — a brain that never rests stops absorbing. Pick your day and protect it.

**The Sunday ritual (20 minutes, every week):**
1. Look at your Gap Log for the week.
2. Plan next week's blocks — which subjects get double time, based on where marks are still bleeding.
3. Parent check-in: one conversation, script provided in the pack. Show them the plan, not the panic.

**Your two tools (print these, back of this pack):**
- **GAP LOG** — three columns: *Topic / Marks lost / Why* (didn't know it · knew it but slipped · ran out of time). One line per practice session.
- **MASTER GAP MAP** — one page, all your subjects ranked by where the most marks are bleeding. You build it in Week 1 and update it every Sunday. From Week 2 onward, this map — not your mood — decides where your hours go.

---

## WEEK 1 · 13 – 19 JULY — AUDIT WEEK
**"You can't fix what you haven't found."**

This week you are not studying. You are diagnosing.

- **Monday to Saturday:** one subject per day. Write one recent past paper for that subject — *untimed*, formula sheets open, no help. Then mark it honestly against the memo and fill in your Gap Log: every question where marks died, and why.
- **Each day's bonus block:** rework the 3 questions that cost you the most marks. Not tomorrow — same day, while the pain is fresh.
- **Sunday:** rest morning. Afternoon: pull all six Gap Logs onto your **Master Gap Map** — every subject, ranked by marks bleeding. Evening: parent check-in #1. The script is one question: *"Show me your Gap Map. Which subject are we attacking hardest in Week 2?"*

**Starting late?** Compress the audit: two subjects a day, your priority subjects first. Do not skip this week — everything after it is aimed by it.

---

## WEEK 2 · 20 – 26 JULY — REPAIR I: YOUR WORST FRONT
**"Double blocks for the biggest bleeder."**

- Your Master Gap Map has a #1 subject. It gets a **double block every study day** this week. Your #2 and #3 subjects share the remaining blocks.
- Repair means: relearn the leaking topic from your class notes or Nexi, then **redo the exact questions you got wrong in Week 1** until they're clean, then find 5 more questions of the same type from another past paper.
- Do not touch your strong subjects this week. They can wait. Marks come from fixing weaknesses, not polishing strengths.
- **Sunday:** update the Gap Map — some topics move off, some move up. Plan Week 3. Parent check-in #2.

**Starting late?** Start here with a one-day mini-audit (one paper in your weakest subject), then follow this week as written.

---

## WEEK 3 · 27 JULY – 2 AUGUST — REPAIR II: THE SECOND FRONT
**"Same method, next target."**

- Subject #2 and #3 on your Gap Map now get the double blocks; #1 drops to maintenance (one block every second day to keep last week's repairs solid).
- Keep the loop: relearn → redo your wrong questions → 5 fresh ones of the same type.
- By Sunday, every subject on your map must have had at least one repair session. If one hasn't, it owns your Sunday afternoon.
- **Sunday:** Gap Map update. Parent check-in #3. This is the halfway mark — the question this week: *"What's off the map since Week 1?"* Celebrate that list. It's real progress, earned.

---

## WEEK 4 · 3 – 9 AUGUST — THE CLOCK ARRIVES
**"Knowing it slowly is not knowing it."**

- Repairs continue, but every day now includes **one timed section**: 45–50 minutes of past-paper questions against the clock, in one subject, no pauses.
- Mark it the same day. New Gap Log column comes alive: *ran out of time*. Time leaks are fixed by drilling question types until they're fast, not by writing faster.
- Rotate subjects so every subject gets at least one timed section this week.
- **Sunday:** Gap Map + plan + check-in #4. Ask yourself honestly: which subject would scare me if the exam were tomorrow? That subject leads Week 5.

---

## WEEK 5 · 10 – 16 AUGUST — FULL PAPERS BEGIN
**"Practise the exam, not just the subject."**

- **Two or three full papers this week**, timed, exam conditions — your scariest subjects first. Same start time as the real exam if you can (fatigue at hour three is a skill you train).
- Mark each paper the same day against the memo. Every lost mark goes in the Gap Log with a reason.
- Non-paper days: light repair on whatever the papers expose.
- **Sunday:** Gap Map + check-in #5. The map should be visibly shorter than Week 1's. Whatever is still stubbornly on it gets named — those items own Week 6's spare blocks.

---

## WEEK 6 · 17 – 23 AUGUST — SIMULATION WEEK
**"Make the real thing feel like a repeat."**

- **Full exam simulations:** real papers, real timing, real conditions — quiet room, no phone, water bottle, watch. Cover your remaining subjects, prioritising anything not yet done as a full paper.
- After each: mark, log, and rework every lost mark within 24 hours. The reworking is where the marks are made — a simulation you don't mark is just tiring.
- Keep evenings light. You are sharpening now, not building.
- **Sunday:** final Gap Map update. Check-in #6: *"What's the plan for taper week?"* — and the answer must include the word *sleep*.

---

## WEEK 7 · 24 – 29 AUGUST — TAPER
**"Protect what you've built."**

- **No new content after Tuesday.** If it isn't in your head by now, defend what is.
- Daily: one light block per upcoming subject — your personal error list (the questions YOU got wrong these 7 weeks), summaries, formula fluency. Nothing heavier.
- Sleep 8 hours. Every night. A rested brain earns more marks than a crammed one — this is the most evidence-backed advice in this entire pack.
- Pack the exam kit the night before each paper: calculator with fresh batteries, ruler, pens, admission details, water.
- The night before each paper, read that subject's **Final 48 Hours** section in its Strategy Sheet. Then close the books.

**You've done the work. Walk in and collect what you've earned.**
