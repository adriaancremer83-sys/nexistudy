## Findings

| # | Claim in sheet | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | P1 and P2 are each **3 hours, 150 marks** — including at prelim level | **CONFIRMED** | [EG] p.4 Table 1 lists "PRELIM EXAM Paper 1 3 hours (150 marks), Paper 2 3 hours (150 marks)" — the guideline itself specifies the prelim. [EC-P1] cover: "MARKS: 150 / TIME: 3 hours". |
| 2 | P1 = "straightforward skills", P2 = "multi-step problems" | **WRONG — corrected in the sheet** | [EG] p.6 Table 3: taxonomy split is IDENTICAL in both papers (L1 30%, L2 30%, L3 20%, L4 20%). The real difference ([EG] p.5 Table 2) is topics: P1 = Finance 60%±5 + Data Handling 35%±5 + Probability 5; P2 = Measurement 55%±5 + Maps & plans 40%±5 + Probability 5 (+±5% linked Finance). This was the pre-2015 paper structure surviving in folklore — exactly the kind of error the pack must not ship. |
| 3 | Combined topic weightings: Finance ±35%, Data ±25%, Measurement ±20%, Maps ±15%, Probability ±5% | **CONFIRMED** | [EG] p.3 "WEIGHTING CAPS NEW 2021 changes" table: Finance 35% (105/300), Data 25% (75), Measurement 20% (60), Maps and plans 15% (45), Probability 5%. |
| 4 | Question 1 in each paper is an easy warm-up | **CONFIRMED (now stated precisely)** | [EG] p.5–6: "in each paper Question 1 (±30 marks) will be based on mixed questions at Taxonomy level 1 only." Sheet now says: Q1 = ±30 marks of pure Level 1. |
| 5 | CA (consistent accuracy) marks follow your method after an error | **CONFIRMED (refined)** | [WCED-MEMO] symbol table: "CA = Consistent accuracy". Standard memo rubric: consistent accuracy applies throughout **but stops at the second calculation error** — the sheet now includes that limit. |
| 6 | "Maths Lit gives you most formulas in the paper" | **PARTLY TRUE — softened** | [EG] taxonomy tables repeatedly say "given formulae" / "formulae provided", BUT p.~10 (Perimeter, area and volume) requires learners to "**Know and use** formulae for perimeter, area and volume." Sheet now says: complex formulas are given in the question, but know perimeter/area/volume cold. |
| 7 | Rounding, units, and showing all working earn/protect marks | **CONFIRMED** | [EC-P1] instructions: "Round off ALL final answers to fit with the content of the question. Write units where needed. … Show ALL calculations." |
| 8 | (New line added) Prelims are set provincially/by school but follow the national format | **CONFIRMED** | Provinces publish their own September preparatory papers ([EC-P1], WC equivalents) in the national format; the final NSC papers are set nationally ([EG] p.4 §2.1). |
