# SUNDAY WHATSAPP SCRIPTS
### NexiStudy Matric Prelim Survival Pack
**Copy, paste, send. Written to not start fights.**

---

## HOW TO USE THESE

One message per Sunday evening, matching the week your child is in. Send it as-is or make it yours — the shape is what matters: **acknowledge → one question → offer something concrete.** Never send more than one. A wall of parent-texts reads as pressure, and pressure subtracts sleep.

---

**WEEK 1 (Sunday 19 July) — after Audit Week:**
> This week looked heavy — well done for getting through the audit. Can you show me your Gap Map tonight? I just want to see which subject we're attacking hardest in Week 2. I'll make your favourite supper while you talk me through it. 💪

**WEEK 2 (Sunday 26 July) — after Repair I:**
> Saw you putting in the double blocks this week. 👊 Quick one for tonight: what's ONE thing that made more sense this week than last week? And anything you need from me for next week — printing, lifts, quiet time?

**WEEK 3 (Sunday 2 Aug) — halfway mark:**
> Halfway. Seriously — halfway. 🎉 Tonight's question: what's OFF the Gap Map since Week 1? I want to hear the list of what you've fixed, not what's left. That list is yours, you earned it.

**WEEK 4 (Sunday 9 Aug) — the clock arrived:**
> Timed sections this week — different animal, hey. Be honest with me tonight: which subject would scare you if the exam was tomorrow? Not to stress about it — just so that one gets first place next week. We handle the scary one head-on.

**WEEK 5 (Sunday 16 Aug) — full papers:**
> Full papers this week. That takes guts — most of your classmates haven't written one yet. Show me the Gap Map tonight? It should be shorter than Week 1's, and I want to see the difference with my own eyes.

**WEEK 6 (Sunday 23 Aug) — after simulation week:**
> You just wrote exam simulations a week before everyone else even starts panicking. 🔥 Tonight's only question: what's the plan for taper week? (Hint: I'd better hear the word "sleep" in the answer.)

**WEEK 7 (during taper week):**
> No questions tonight. Just this: I've watched you work for seven weeks, and whatever happens in there, you've already done something most people can't. Early night. Go collect what you've earned. ❤️

## SPARE SCRIPTS (for the moments in between)

**The night before any paper:**
> Kit packed? Calculator batteries? Good. No more books tonight — your Strategy Sheet says so, not just me. Sleep is marks. See you at breakfast.

**After a paper that went badly:**
> Heard today was rough. Not doing a post-mortem at the door — supper's ready and that's all tonight is. The next paper matters more than the last one, and Sunday's Gap Map can hear the rest.

**On the rest day (if you can't help yourself):**
> Enjoy the day off. That's it. That's the message. 😄
